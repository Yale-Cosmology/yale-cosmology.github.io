Thank you for joining the Yale Cosmology Seminar. We aim for a welcoming, informal discussion of cosmology, with plenty of room for questions and ideas.

## Preparing your talk

### Length and discussion

Please prepare approximately **45 minutes of presentation**, with time for discussion in addition. Leave room for questions along the way, and confirm the full session length with your host when planning your visit.

### Audience and background

Aim your introduction at graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and faculty working across cosmology and related areas. Advanced undergraduates may also attend. Explain the motivation and essential background before moving into specialist results; avoid assuming familiarity with your particular subfield.

Introduce unfamiliar notation, define technical terms, and explain what your methods measure and why they matter. Theory, observations, simulations, and statistical methods all benefit from a clear physical starting point.

### An informal atmosphere

Think of the seminar as a conversation with colleagues. Audience questions and short discussions are welcome throughout. You are encouraged to pause, invite questions, and make space for ideas that emerge during the talk.

## Before your visit

Please send your host your **name, affiliation, talk title, abstract, and a headshot** for the announcement. Let them know about any accessibility needs or presentation requirements.

### Arrival and presentation setup

Your host will confirm the building, room, arrival arrangements, and available presentation equipment. Check whether you should bring a laptop, adapter, or another way to share your slides. Refer to your talk announcement for the advertised date, time, and location.

### Meetings and travel

If you would like to meet particular people during your visit, tell your host in advance. They can help coordinate meetings and provide current local travel guidance.

### Expenses

Confirm any reimbursement arrangements with your host before making travel purchases. Your host can explain which expenses are covered and who handles the paperwork.

## Questions

Contact the organizer or host who invited you. They can confirm the details specific to your session.
