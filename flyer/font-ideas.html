Key science targets achievable by a next-generation ultradeep, ultrahigh-resolution CMB survey
Date  Tuesday, October 20, 2026  ·  Time  1:30 PM  ·  Location  KT 401
Measurements of the cosmic microwave background (CMB) have placed increasingly tight constraints on cosmological parameters, and measurements of CMB temperature and polarization power spectra (TT, TE, EE) by current experiments such as the Simons Observatory are expected to be sample-variance limited out to multipoles of 1000. We forecast how a next-generation ultradeep, ultrahigh-resolution CMB survey, such as CMB-HD, will improve the constraints on our cosmological model by measuring CMB temperature and polarization, along with the lensing of the CMB, on much smaller scales, out to multipoles of 20,000. In particular, we obtain a one-sigma error of 0.0154 on the effective number of relativistic species.
