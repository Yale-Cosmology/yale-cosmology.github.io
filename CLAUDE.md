# Rules for this repository

## Never change the words of a talk's title or abstract

Titles and abstracts belong to the speakers. Keep their wording exactly as provided, everywhere they appear: the talk files in `src/content/talks/`, the flyer templates in `flyer/`, and anything else.

Do not replace, add, or remove words, and do not reword, paraphrase, or correct spelling or grammar.

Formatting and typographic changes are fine, such as splitting an abstract into paragraphs, typesetting symbols (for example "LCDM" as "ΛCDM" or "H0" with a subscript), proper dashes and quotation marks, and HTML markup. If a title or abstract looks wrong, point it out and ask; do not edit the words.

## Report typos and grammar problems

When adding or touching a title or abstract, read it for typos, grammar mistakes, and inconsistent capitalization, and always tell the user what you found, quoting the passage and suggesting a fix. Leave the text unchanged; the user decides whether to ask the speaker.
