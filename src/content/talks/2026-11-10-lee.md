title: Field-Level Baryonic Halo Painting for Weak Lensing

Baryonic feedback is a leading systematic uncertainty for Stage-IV weak lensing surveys such as LSST, Euclid, and Roman. I will present BIND, a conditional flow-matching model that paints dark matter, gas, and stellar mass onto dark-matter-only halos. BIND takes as input the full 35-parameter cosmological and IllustrisTNG feedback space, allowing it to generate fields as functions of feedback and cosmology. On held-out simulations, it recovers integrated masses at the percent level, radial profiles to within 10%, and halo shapes and baryon fractions. Applied halo by halo to an N-body volume, BIND reproduces the power-spectrum suppression of the hydrodynamical halos it replaces, in minutes on one GPU.

I will then show ray-traced convergence, optical-depth and Compton-*y* lightcones spanning the full feedback prior. At fixed cosmology, that prior is a 10–22σ effect for LSST-Y10. Different statistics respond to different feedback channels, and seven measurable halo properties predict every statistic through a single linear map. I will close with how these results open a path to marginalizing over feedback and to joint models of baryons and intrinsic alignments.
