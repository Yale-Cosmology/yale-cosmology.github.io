The seminar meets in **KT 401**, on the fourth floor of Kline Tower, on Yale's Science Hill. Visitors are welcome; there is no registration.

## Finding the room

Enter Kline Tower from the plaza on the Sachem Street side, at the southeast corner of the building (marked "Enter here" on the map). Take the elevators to the fourth floor; KT 401 is the seminar room near the elevators. If the door is locked, email one of the organizers listed below and someone will let you in.

## Parking

The map above shows the options closest to Kline Tower, based on Yale's visitor parking list and the City of New Haven's parking meter and residential zone map.

- **Yale visitor lots** (P markers): the nearest is Lot 26V at 200 Prospect Street, directly across from Kline Tower. Lot 37V at 83 Trumbull Street is a few blocks south, and Lot 12V at 433 Prospect Street and 2 Science Park at 50 Division Street are a little further up the hill. All are paid, and 26V and 37V are free after 5 PM. Details and payment instructions are on [Yale's visitor parking page](https://your.yale.edu/campus-essentials/getting-around-campus/parking/visitor-parking).
- **Metered street parking** (orange): the closest meters are on Prospect Street just north of Sachem Street, with no time limit, and on Sachem Street, Hillhouse Avenue, Trumbull Street, and Whitney Avenue south of Sachem, most with a five-hour limit. Pay at the meter or with the ParkMobile app. City meters run 8 AM to 9 PM, Monday through Saturday; posted time limits are enforced until 5 PM. Sundays are free.
- **Free street parking** (green): Whitney Avenue north of Lawrence Street is unmetered, and the East Rock blocks east of it around Livingston Street, between Lawrence Street and Cold Spring Street, are outside any permit zone. From there it is a ten- to fifteen-minute walk back along Whitney Avenue and Sachem Street.
- **Residential permit zones** (blue, dashed): the East Rock streets south of Lawrence Street, between Whitney Avenue and State Street, and the blocks west of Prospect Street around Tilton, Woodland, and Compton Streets are residential permit zones. Parking on the signed blocks there needs a resident permit during the posted hours.

If you are a visiting speaker, your host can arrange parking for you; just ask.

## By train

New Haven's Union Station is served by Amtrak, Metro-North from Grand Central, and Shore Line East. From the station, Kline Tower is about two miles north: a ten-minute taxi or ride-share, or take the Yale Shuttle or a CTtransit bus toward Science Hill and get off at Prospect and Sachem.

## By car

Take I-91 to Exit 3 (Trumbull Street) and follow Trumbull Street west to Whitney Avenue or Prospect Street, then turn right. See the parking section above for where to leave the car.

## Accessibility

Kline Tower is step-free from the Sachem Street entrance, and the fourth floor is reachable by elevator. Let an organizer know in advance if you need any other accommodation.
